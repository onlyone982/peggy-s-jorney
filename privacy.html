Privacy Policy

peggy's journey · Last updated: 2026-11-19

1. What this app is

peggy's journey is a free endless-runner game. You can play it in a web browser at https://peggy.day and as an Android app. You do not need an account, and the game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Information we collect

  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics 4 / Firebase). The game sends anonymous events such as "game started", "game over" (with the score and the number of fish collected), "share button used", and "sound muted". Google Analytics also receives technical information such as an approximate region (derived from the IP address), device and browser type, language, and an app/browser identifier. We use this only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. Advertising features and ad personalization are turned off. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, analytics storage is denied by default. The game also respects the browser "Do Not Track" setting.
  • Data stored on your device. Your high score, the number of games you played, and the sound on/off setting are stored locally on your device (local storage). This data is not sent to us.

3. How we use information

Only to measure and improve the game. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

4. Third parties

Google LLC provides Google Analytics. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The Android app does not currently show third-party ads.

5. Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

You can clear the locally stored data by clearing the app data or browser data. You can turn on "Do Not Track" in your browser to disable analytics in the web version.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

1. 서비스 소개

peggy's journey는 무료 러너 게임이며, 웹 브라우저(https://peggy.day)와 안드로이드 앱에서 즐길 수 있습니다. 계정이 필요 없고, 이름·이메일·전화번호·위치 정보를 요구하지 않습니다.

2. 수집하는 정보

  • 이용 통계(Google Analytics 4 / Firebase): 게임 시작, 게임 종료(점수와 먹은 생선 수), 공유 버튼 사용, 소리 끄기 같은 익명 이벤트를 전송합니다. Google Analytics는 IP 주소에서 추정한 대략적인 지역, 기기·브라우저 종류, 언어, 앱/브라우저 식별자 같은 기술 정보도 받습니다. 게임을 어떻게 즐기는지 파악하고 개선하는 데에만 사용하며, 광고 기능과 광고 개인화는 꺼 두었습니다. 유럽경제지역·영국·스위스에서는 동의 전까지 분석 저장을 거부하는 것이 기본값이고, 브라우저의 '추적 안 함' 설정도 따릅니다.
  • 기기에 저장되는 정보: 최고 점수, 플레이한 판 수, 소리 설정은 기기 안(로컬 저장소)에만 저장되며 저희에게 전송되지 않습니다.
